# Code of Conduct

We want Temper to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone.

**Expected behaviour:** be respectful and patient, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, assume good intent, and focus on what is best for the project and community.

**Unacceptable behaviour:** harassment, insults or personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing others' private information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that violate this code, and may ban contributors who do so repeatedly.

To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting on the Security tab or email mateistf13@gmail.com. Reports are handled confidentially.

This policy is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
